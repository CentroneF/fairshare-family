## Notes

Responsive dashboard navigation: on smaller screens, secondary dashboard
information is available through a slide-in burger-menu drawer; on larger
screens, it is presented in a sidebar. The existing first-time and
awaiting-second-parent setup screens remain unchanged. Selecting a past report
from the small-screen drawer closes that drawer. The drawer also closes through
its close button or by tapping its backdrop. Navigation links to a dedicated
report-history page, which lists clickable reports with their month, settlement
status, and approved total.
